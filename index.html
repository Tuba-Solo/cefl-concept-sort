CEFL 7–9 Concept Sort

CEFL is one course that runs for three years. All three grades share the same three organizing ideas, and each grade has its own outcomes inside them. Each table places its own cards on one board the whole room shares.

  1. 1 Choose your table. Your cards are your table's colour.
  2. 2 Tap one of your cards.
  3. 3 Tap Place here in the box for the grade and organizing idea where it belongs.
  4. 4 To move a card, tap it, then tap Place here in its new box.
  5. 5 Press Check our cards to see which of your cards are in the right box. Move any marked ✗ and check again.
  6. 6 Your facilitator reveals the answers for the whole room.

Which table are you at?

You'll get your table's cards. Everyone at your table shares them.

Clear the whole board? Every table's cards go back to their trays.
